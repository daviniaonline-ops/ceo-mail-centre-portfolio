Your 15-minute daily run

  1. Tap Sync new activity on your laptop (Claude desktop app) to pull in new messages, comments and followers. On your phone, open an inbox, copy the screen and use + Log new, then Paste a screen.
  2. Clear Reply now first. Real conversations beat cold welcomes.
  3. Work through Welcome: draft, tweak, copy, send in the app, tap Mark sent.
  4. Send the Follow-ups due. After the second follow-up the person drops to Gone quiet. Stop is a valid outcome.
  5. Tap They replied the moment someone answers, so they jump back to Reply now.
